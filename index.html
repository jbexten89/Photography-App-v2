                              Column Widths

                              Sales Row Highlight

                              Tint each Sales-account income row green so they stand out at a glance.

                              Job-Linked Expense Highlight

                              Tint expense rows that are tied to a Job No. red so they're easy to spot.

                              Color Rows by Job No.

                              Tint each row in the donut color of its Job No. (only takes effect when both green Sales and red job-expense highlights above are off).

                              Locked Years

                              Lock a tax year so transactions and invoices in that year can't be edited or deleted. Useful once you've filed.

                              Snapshot History

                              Cloud-stored copies of your entire data state. Created automatically (max once per hour) and on demand. Up to 30 kept; oldest pruned. Restore any snapshot if a change ever turns out wrong — a "Pre-Restore" snapshot of current state is taken automatically before the restore, so you can always come back.
