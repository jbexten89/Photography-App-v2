1. App Overview

This app runs entirely in your browser. No server, no login — just open the page and your data is already loaded from the local device. It's designed for a sole-proprietor photography business to track every dollar in and out, produce invoices, log business mileage, and surface clean financial reports at tax time.

What the app tracks

  • Income & expenses per transaction, tagged to a Customer, Category (Job), and Payee.
  • Invoices with line items, optional sales tax, and direct links to the income transactions they cover.
  • Mileage trips with IRS-rate deduction.
  • Scheduled photo shoots (Calendar) that feed the Un Invoiced queue.
  • Savings progress toward a configurable goal.

Where the data lives

All state is stored in localStorage under the key photo-bookkeeping-v1. Each browser/device keeps its own copy — use the built-in backup tools (Settings → Data) to copy the data elsewhere, including iCloud Drive on Apple devices.

2. Sidebar Navigation

The left sidebar (or the hamburger menu on mobile) lists the main tabs:

  • Overview — financial at-a-glance for the year (dashboard).
  • Calendar — scheduled photo shoots.
  • Analytics — two sub-tabs: Breakdown and Trends.
  • All Transactions — the full income/expense ledger.
  • Invoices — builder, list, and Un Invoiced queue.
  • Mileage — business trip tracker.
  • Reports — Profit & Loss, customer sales, sales tax.
  • Settings — bottom gear icon; data, customization, backups.

Mobile navigation styles

Settings → Mobile Nav Style lets you switch between two modes:

  • Sidebar (default): hamburger opens the same left drawer as desktop.
  • Bottom Bar: a fixed 5-slot bottom toolbar — Overview, Transactions, Calendar, Analytics, More. Tapping More slides a right-side flyout containing every other tab plus the light/dark theme toggle and Settings.

Theme toggle

A moon/sun icon at the bottom of the sidebar (or inside the mobile More flyout) flips between dark and light themes. Your choice is remembered per device.

3. Overview Tab (Dashboard)

The Overview gives you the big picture for whichever year you pick.

Year selector

Pill buttons across the top let you filter by year or choose All. On desktop, every year with data appears as its own pill. On mobile you see All + the three most-recent years + a More ▾ button; tapping More opens a bottom sheet with every available year as a grid.

Savings Goal bar

The goal amount is configured in Settings. The bar fills as your Savings-category transactions accumulate. On desktop it renders as a vertical column; on mobile it rotates into a horizontal strip so it doesn't dominate the screen. A thin accent line marks the target — fill past it and the bar turns solid green (Goal met).

Total Income by Year

A bar chart showing up to 6 years at a time. Use the ‹ / › arrows at the top of the card to slide the window. Each bar is labeled with its total and a percentage-change delta vs. the previous year. Toggle Full Year / Year to Date to switch the reference.

Income by Job donut

Shows the share each job contributed to your income for the selected year. The center displays the total. The legend lists every job with its percentage. Hover a slice to highlight it (desktop); on mobile, tap the legend to focus a slice.

Summary cards

Five stat tiles: Total Income, Total Expenses, Total Savings, Net, and the Transactions count.

Summary tables

Three tables sit below the charts:

  • By Job — income-positive categories, with Income, Expenses, Net, Count.
  • By Category — expense-only and savings categories, with Expenses, Income, Net, Count.
  • By Account — aggregated per account.

On mobile the first two columns (e.g. Job + Income) are visible at the edge; slide the table left to reveal the rest. Desktop shows all columns at once.

Drill-through: on mobile, long-press any row in the By Job or By Category table to open that category's transactions with a Back arrow that returns you to the Overview. On desktop, a single click does the same.

4. Calendar Tab

Log every scheduled photo shoot here so nothing slips through the cracks.

Year navigation

The sticky top bar contains: ‹ year › arrows to jump years, a Today button that snaps to the current year, and a + New Event button on the right (inline on mobile so everything fits one row).

Adding a new event

  1. Tap + New Event.
  2. Fill out Date (required), Time, Job, Location, and Notes.
  3. Save. The event appears in the Scheduled Jobs list below, sorted by date.

Editing / deleting an event

Click any event row to reopen the modal — change values and Save, or click Delete to remove it.

Every event you schedule feeds the Un Invoiced queue on the Invoices tab when a paid transaction for that job exists but hasn't been linked to an invoice yet.

5. Analytics Tab

Two sub-tabs sit sticky at the top: Breakdown and Trends. Switch freely — they share the same sidebar selection.

Breakdown

Pick a year from the pills. Three donuts summarize the slice — Jobs (positive-net categories), Expenses, and Savings. Empty buckets hide themselves so an unused year shows only the relevant donuts.

Below the donuts is a grid of job cards. Each card shows the category name, income, expenses, and net. Use the Gross | Net toggle on the right (Gross is default) to flip how the Jobs donut and totals calculate.

Click (desktop) or long-press (mobile) a job card to jump to the Transactions tab filtered to that category + year. A Back arrow in the Transactions toolbar brings you straight back to Analytics.

Trends

Trends draws a historical bar chart. The toolbar controls what you're looking at:

  • View By — Year (single year, all jobs stacked) or Job (one job across years).
  • Year — multi-select when Job view is active; single-select in Year view. Defaults to All Years in Job mode.
  • Jobs — pick which category drives the chart in Job mode.
  • Payees — filter by payee (defaults to Job for photography income).

In Job mode every bar is labeled with the dollar amount and % change vs. the previous year (green for growth, red for decline). A legend below the chart shows each segment's color and contribution.

6. All Transactions Tab

The full ledger. Sort, filter, search, edit, reconcile — everything lives here.

Top bar

The running Selected count and Working Balance sit in a sticky bar at the very top so they stay visible while you scroll. Working Balance reflects your current filter set — narrow the list and the number updates instantly.

Toolbar

  • + New Transaction — opens the form.
  • All Years / All Categories / All Types — dropdown filters.
  • Find (magnifying glass) — click the icon to expand an inline search box. It matches across payee, memo, vendor, customer, category, tags, and amount. Click the icon again to collapse and clear.
  • ⋯ menu — opens the Reconcile modal, toggles Select Mode, and hides/shows reconciled rows.

Table columns

Checkbox • Date • Vendor • Customer • Payee • Category • Amount • Memo • R (padlock) • Tag icon. The old Account column is removed — the app uses a single default account under the hood.

Padlock (R) states

  • Dim open padlock — uncleared.
  • Accent open padlock — cleared (C).
  • Primary closed padlock — reconciled (R).

Click the padlock to cycle through the three states.

Tag icon

The tag icon dims when the row has no tags and lights up in the primary color when it does. Hover for a tooltip listing the tags.

Adding a transaction

  1. Click + New Transaction.
  2. Date is required. Vendor is optional.
  3. Customer is a dropdown of previously-saved names. Pick + Add new… to be prompted for a new value. Customer is required when Payee is Job; optional otherwise.
  4. Payee is required. Category is required.
  5. Fill the Outflow (expense) or Inflow (income) amount — not both. The swap arrow flips the two fields if you typed in the wrong one.
  6. Memo and Tags are optional.
  7. Save. The transaction appears in the list immediately.

Editing / duplicating / deleting

Click any row to open the edit modal. The Duplicate button clones the transaction with a fresh ID (but today's date); the Delete button removes it with a confirmation prompt.

Reconciling against a bank statement

  1. Open the ⋯ menu → Reconcile…
  2. Enter the statement date, the ending balance, and pick the account.
  3. Check off every transaction that appears on the statement. The Difference must reach $0.00.
  4. Click Finalize to mark every checked row as Reconciled (R).
On mobile the instructions auto-hide — tap the small ? beside the title to toggle them. Cancel and Finalize live at the top-right so they stay in reach while you scroll the transaction list.

7. Invoices Tab

Build, send, and track invoices — and tie each one to the income transactions it represents so your books stay clean.

Summary cards

Total Invoiced, Total Paid, Outstanding. All three honor the year filter — narrow to 2026 and only 2026 invoices count.

Un Invoiced card

Lists income transactions (from the current-filter year) that aren't linked to any invoice yet. Columns: Date, Customer, Category, Amount, ascending by date. When every qualifying transaction has an invoice link, the card hides automatically.

Invoices list

Sorted by invoice number descending. Columns: Invoice # • Total • Date • Bill To • Job • Status (Paid/Unpaid pill) • Linked (chain icon with a blue count). On mobile, Total is hidden (tap the invoice to see it); the other columns get wider real estate and the table slides horizontally.

Auto-numbering rules

  • First invoice of a new year: YY001 (e.g. 26001 for 2026).
  • Subsequent new invoices fill the lowest gap in the current-year sequence.
  • Deleting an invoice frees its number for reuse.
  • Auto-numbers never drop below the current year's floor (e.g. won't re-use 25099).
  • Manual override: type any unused number. Duplicates are blocked at save time.

Creating or editing an invoice

  1. Click + New Invoice (or a row in the list).
  2. Confirm the auto-populated Invoice # or type your own.
  3. Set the Date, Bill To (multi-line — the first line becomes the Customer), and Job.
  4. Leave tax as NON-TAX (default) or flip to Tax; for Tax, set label (e.g. Ohio Sales Tax) and rate.
  5. Add line items: Item, Qty, Description, Price. Amount auto-calculates.
  6. Link transactions (see below).
  7. Click Save. Use Print / PDF to produce a file to send the client.

Linking transactions to an invoice

The Linked Transactions panel sits at the top of the editor, above the printable sheet (it's no-print so it won't appear on PDFs).

  1. Click + Link Transactions… inside that panel.
  2. The modal is already filtered to candidates whose Category matches the invoice's Job AND whose Customer matches the first line of Bill To.
  3. Check the rows that make up this invoice's payment. The Customer column on the right shows any other invoice already linking each row (helpful for avoiding double-links).
  4. Save the modal. The panel lists the selected rows with totals; deleted transactions are pruned on the next save so the Linked count stays accurate.

Marking paid / deleting

Mark as Paid toggles the invoice's paid state (and stamps a paid date). The list immediately reflects the new status pill. Delete removes the invoice — a confirmation prompts first.

8. Mileage Tab

Log business trips so the IRS-rate deduction is just a click away at tax time.

Year summary

Shows total miles, total deduction (miles × IRS rate from Settings), and trip count for the selected year.

Adding a trip

  1. Click + New Trip.
  2. Enter Date, From, To, Purpose.
  3. Either type Miles directly, or fill Start/End odometer and the miles auto-calculate.
  4. Save. The trip appears in the list sorted by date.

Click a trip row to edit or delete.

9. Reports Tab

Canned reports you can hand to your accountant:

  • Profit & Loss — income, expenses, and net for the selected year.
  • Sales Tax Liability — breaks down taxable / non-taxable invoiced amounts and total tax collected.
  • Customer Sales Summary — total revenue per customer.

Every report respects the year filter at the top of the page. Use your browser's Print function to save any report as a PDF.

10. Settings Tab

Organized into cards. On mobile the Data card floats to the top for one-tap access to backups.

Data card

  • Startup View — Overview or All Transactions.
  • Overview Year — Current Year or All Years when the app first opens.
  • Savings Goal ($) — drives the goal bar on Overview.
  • Download Backup (JSON) — native save dialog; file name includes date + time.
  • Backup to iCloud — remembered-folder backup (see next page).
  • Restore from Backup — import any JBPhoto-*.json file.
  • Import MoneyStats CSV — bulk-load transactions from a MoneyStats export.
  • App Info — shows live line-of-code counts for app.js, styles.css, and index.html.
  • Delete Transactions in Date Range — type yes to confirm.
  • Clear All Data — wipes everything in localStorage; also confirmation-gated.

Accounts card

Manage the list of bank accounts / cash buckets. (The app operates on a single default account today; this list is retained for future multi-account support.)

Chart of Accounts card

Accounting-style categories. Each transaction can optionally be assigned to a chart account for formal P&L rollups. Add, edit, and nest sub-accounts here.

11. Backup & Restore

Because every piece of data lives in your browser's localStorage, regular backups are essential. The app gives you two paths.

Download Backup (JSON)

Click the button; your browser's native save dialog opens, starting in the Documents folder. The default filename is JBPhoto-backup-YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS.json, so successive saves never overwrite each other. Navigate anywhere you like — including iCloud Drive — and save.

Backup to iCloud (recommended)

  1. On the first click you'll be asked to pick a folder. Browse to iCloud Drive › JBPhoto (create the folder if it doesn't exist) and grant the permission.
  2. The app stores the folder reference in your browser (IndexedDB).
  3. Every subsequent click writes straight to that folder with a fresh date-stamped filename — no dialog, no clicks.
  4. If you clear browser data or switch devices, the first click will prompt you to choose the folder again.
Firefox and Safari don't yet support the folder-pick API. On those browsers the button tells you to use Download Backup (JSON) instead and pick iCloud Drive from the native save dialog.

Restoring a backup

  1. Click Restore from Backup.
  2. Select any JBPhoto-*.json file.
  3. Confirm the overwrite prompt.
  4. The app replaces current state but preserves every field in the backup — transactions, invoices, chart accounts, vendors, customers, mileage trips, scheduled jobs, savings goal.

12. Tips & Tricks

  • Drill-through navigation: long-press (mobile) or click (desktop) a row in Overview's By Job / By Category tables to open its transactions. The Back arrow on the Transactions toolbar returns you to the exact tab you came from.
  • Analytics job cards support the same long-press / double-click behavior — a single tap on mobile selects the card, a second tap within half a second opens the drill-through.
  • Sticky top bars on mobile: year/status bars on Overview, the Selected + Working Balance bar on Transactions, and the Breakdown/Trends tabs on Analytics all stay put while you scroll.
  • Customer autocomplete: every customer name you've ever entered appears in the dropdown. Pick an existing name or add a new one without leaving the form.
  • Unified Find: one search box hunts across payee, memo, vendor, customer, category, tags, and amount. Type a dollar figure to find transactions at that price.
  • Invoice auto-numbering always fills gaps first. Delete 26004 and the next new invoice reuses 26004; once current-year numbers are fully used, it rolls forward to 26005, 26006, etc.
  • Linked invoice filter: the + Link Transactions modal is pre-filtered by the invoice's Bill To (Customer) and Job (Category), so you rarely need to search.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Esc closes any open modal; standard browser shortcuts (Cmd/Ctrl+F, Cmd/Ctrl+P) work as expected.
  • Safe exit: every destructive action (clear data, delete range, delete invoice) requires a typed confirmation. You can't accidentally wipe your books.
